# Colibri Stateless contributor privacy notice
27 November 2026

This notice explains how corpus.core GmbH processes personal data to administer the Contributor License Agreement (CLA). It is not part of the CLA and does not ask for consent to marketing.

## Controller and contact
corpus.core GmbH, Markt 16, 09648 Mittweida, Germany. Contact for CLA and data protection requests: jork@corpuscore.tech.

## Data and purposes
We process your GitHub numeric account ID and login, the name and contact email you supply, the accepted agreement version and its identifying revision, acceptance time, the relevant repository and contributions, and any rights-holder details or authorization you provide. We use this information to conclude and administer the CLA, check contribution coverage, contact you about rights issues and document our entitlement to use contributions. Where needed we separately record a content checksum and supporting authorization evidence.

The legal bases are Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for concluding and administering the agreement and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for documenting the chain of rights, managing corporate authorizations and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Our legitimate interest is reliable evidence of the rights in the Project. Statutory recordkeeping, where applicable, is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

## Services and recipients
The signing process uses GitHub and the hosted CLA Assistant service provided by SAP. These services receive data needed for authentication, acceptance records and PR status checks and process service-related data under their applicable privacy notices. Authorized project administrators can access acceptance records. Where necessary, relevant evidence may be shared with legal advisers, courts, authorities or a successor to the Project for the purposes described above.

Your public GitHub login and whether the required CLA check has passed may be visible in the public pull request. Do not post your legal name, contact email or private authorization documents in public comments. The public agreement Gist contains the agreement and blank form definitions, not your completed identity fields. We do not publish completed identity fields or private authorization documents in the public repository.

Provider notices, including details of international processing and applicable transfer safeguards:
- GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- CLA Assistant and SAP: https://gist.github.com/CLAassistant/3a73e4cd729c9d0a6e30

## Retention
We retain the minimum acceptance and rights evidence for as long as necessary to document rights in contributions we continue to use or distribute, and to meet applicable statutory duties or resolve legal claims. Stopping contributions does not by itself remove the need to retain evidence of existing licenses. We review the necessity and scope of retention and remove data that is no longer needed. Service providers apply their own retention rules to data they process for their own purposes.

## Your rights
Subject to the GDPR's conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Erasure may be limited where data remains necessary for legal claims or statutory obligations. Contact jork@corpuscore.tech to exercise these rights.

You may complain to a supervisory authority, including the Saxon Commissioner for Data Protection and Transparency: https://www.datenschutz.sachsen.de/.

## Required information and automated checks
The identity and contact fields are needed to administer the CLA. Without the necessary information, we cannot complete the ordinary signing process or merge a contribution that requires the CLA; contact us if you need an alternative process. Automated checks compare contribution authors with acceptance records and report a merge status. We do not use this information for profiling or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely through these checks. Employer authority, guardian consent and disputed rights require human review.

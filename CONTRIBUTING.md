# Contributing to Colibri Stateless

Contributions are welcome through GitHub pull requests.

## Contributor License Agreement
Before your first contribution can be merged, accept our [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA bot posts a [signing link](https://cla-assistant.io/corpus-core/colibri-stateless) in your pull request. Sign in with your GitHub account, review the agreement and accept it once. A new agreement version requires fresh acceptance.

Every contributing author, including co-authors, must be covered. The required CLA status check is supplemented by maintainer review of co-author coverage and any employer or guardian authorizations.

The CLA governs; this summary is not part of it:
- You keep your copyright and remain free to use your contributions elsewhere.
- corpus.core GmbH receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free license, including commercial and proprietary sublicensing. No contractual payment or revenue share is promised; mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.
- You grant a patent license limited to your contribution and the project combination to which you submit it.
- Rights already granted continue after you stop contributing, subject to mandatory statutory rights.

Earlier contributions are covered only if expressly included in your acceptance record or a separate agreement (CLA section 1.2).

## Employer and client rights
If your employer or client owns rights in your contribution, it must authorize the contribution. Provide authorization and evidence of authority privately to jork@corpuscore.tech for verification before merge. For entities with several contributors, we arrange a corporate contributor agreement covering authorized contributors.

If you are under 18, contact jork@corpuscore.tech privately. We must separately receive and verify your parent or legal guardian's consent in text form before accepting your CLA or merging your contribution.

## Third-party material and license compliance
Review contributions for license compliance before submission. Clearly identify third-party material, its source, license and required notices. Material under compatible permissive licenses such as MIT, BSD or Apache 2.0 can be included with that identification.

Do not include material under copyleft licenses such as GPL, AGPL or LGPL, proprietary licenses or other terms that conflict with the CLA. Disclose the material and its obligations, including source publication or commercial-use restrictions. Inclusion requires explicit Company approval in text form; approval never overrides third-party license terms.

Every file, addition and modification proposed in your pull request is part of your Contribution. A notice such as "Not a Contribution" cannot exclude it from the CLA.

If AI tools generated a substantial part of a contribution, disclose that in the pull request and review the output like any other code.

## Confidential information
Do not submit confidential information or material you are not entitled to contribute.

## Privacy
Acceptance records include your numeric GitHub account ID, login at acceptance, name and contact email. Provide personal identity details and authorizations privately, not in public PR comments. We do not publish those completed fields in the public repository.

Read the [contributor privacy notice](CLA_PRIVACY.md) before signing.
